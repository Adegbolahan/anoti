# Experiment: Q001 Format Comprehension (pre-registered)

**Status:** DESIGN, pre-registered — metrics and decision rule frozen before
any run; results filed under docs/trials/2026-08-13-q001-formats/.
**Claims under test:** Q001 (does familiar syntax carrying agent-oriented
content design beat v1-era YAML and sigil notation on comprehension
accuracy AND token cost?); evidence lands on D004.

## Method

The benchmark's 8 item-tracker facts (2 with revisions) encoded four ways,
information-identical including revision history: `v1-era YAML` (flat
summary entries), `v3 YAML` (typed records + events), `agent-Markdown`
(claim-first with index), `sigil` (bespoke ⟦⟧◆ notation). One fresh haiku
reader per format — same model, same 10 exact-answer questions, no other
context, blinded to the experiment. n=1 per format: pilot-grade, stated.

## Metrics

- **Accuracy:** questions correct of 10, graded mechanically against the
  key (exact-match after normalization; partial credit 0.5 where the key
  marks it).
- **Cost proxy:** file size in bytes and chars/4 approximate tokens
  (tokenizer-exact counts unavailable here; approximation stated).

## Decision rule (frozen)

Q001 answers YES if v3 YAML or agent-Markdown (a) scores ≥ each of v1 and
sigil on accuracy AND (b) measures smaller than sigil and within 15% of
v1 on the cost proxy. Any other pattern: NO or MIXED, recorded as-is.
Closure of Q001 is proposed to the human either way; a pilot moves
nothing to established.

## Cost estimate

Four haiku dispatches, ~10-15k tokens total. Trivial.
