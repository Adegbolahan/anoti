# Policy: draft-for-ratification

**Applies:** roles that declare it — work whose output is direction,
values, or anything targeting a human-owned organ (ROADMAP.md,
HIGH-LEVEL-STORIES.md).

**Procedure:**

1. Your output is a **draft**: clearly marked as proposed, never applied
   directly to a human-owned artifact. Ownership does not transfer to you.
2. Structure the draft for a decision, not a reading: what you propose,
   what it replaces, the trade-off you weighed (cited where factual,
   labeled judgment where opinion).
3. Hand the draft to the main session; the human ratifies, amends, or
   rejects. Ratification is understanding — per the slogan, it is never
   outsourced, never inferred from silence, and in human-absent contexts
   it queues rather than defaults to yes.

**Binds:** the cascade's roadmap and stories gates; the human-absent queue.

**Violation handling:** a direct edit to a human-owned organ is reverted
and re-issued as a draft, whatever its quality.
